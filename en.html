About

I’m Hongpeng Bai. I study Android security at the School of Cybersecurity, Tianjin University, under the supervision of Prof. Guangquan Xu. My research asks how reliably we can detect malware and gray-market apps as applications, malware families, and dataset labels change over time.

  • Datasets and labels. I trace malware family labels back to expert reports and study how to identify and correct labeling errors in third-party datasets.
  • Out-of-distribution detection. I evaluate how detectors handle Android malware and gray-market apps that differ from their training samples.
  • Malware family classification. I study how classifiers can learn a new malware family from a few samples while retaining what they know about earlier families.
